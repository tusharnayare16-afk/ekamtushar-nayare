Audit Verification Protocol

Objective: Guidelines for citizens and auditors verifying public road construction in Bhopal district.

Step 1: Obtain Project Reference Code

Locate the specific PMGSY or PWD package number on the road sign board or JanData road list.

Step 2: Cross-Reference GeoSadak GIS

Open GeoSadak Open Data portal. Match spatial coordinates with mapped asphalt stretches.

Step 3: Review Tender Financial Disclosures

Check MP e-Procurement portal tenders to compare awarded contract value against official expenditure logs.
